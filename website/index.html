Framework

4-Strategy Fine-Tuning

All experiments compare four strategies on the same limited target-building data to isolate the effect of pre-training.

Scratch (Control)

  • Purpose: Baseline control group trained from scratch.
  • Architecture: 2 layers × 64 hidden LSTM.
  • Initialisation: Random weights.
  • Trainable Params: ~88 K (all).

Full Fine-Tuning

  • Purpose: Warm-start fine-tuning on all parameters.
  • Architecture: 2 layers × 64 hidden LSTM.
  • Initialisation: Pre-trained baseline weights.
  • Learning Rate: 1e-4 (10× lower than Scratch).

Frozen Backbone

  • Purpose: Prevent catastrophic forgetting in low-data regimes.
  • Architecture: LSTM frozen; only MLP head trainable.
  • Initialisation: Pre-trained baseline weights.
  • Trainable Params: ~8 K.

Adapter Layers

  • Purpose: Minimal-parameter transfer with added expressiveness.
  • Architecture: 32-dim bottleneck adapter, LSTM frozen.
  • Initialisation: Pre-trained baseline weights.
  • Trainable Params: ~16 K.
